Add-ons, sealed off from your PC

Add-ons run inside Ingle in a sandbox. They can't touch your files or the internet, and they only do what you allow. The first one, Dice, rolls in chat: /roll 2d6+1.

Make your own with Claude

Add Ingle's Claude connector in Settings → Claude, and Claude can build a skin or a plugin with you, show the skin on your Ingle while you talk it over, and send it to the Ingle Library, where it's reviewed before anyone else sees it. The plugin kit has everything Claude needs.

Checked every time

Ingle checks every file against the library's signed list before it installs a plugin or a skin, and again each time a program opens.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Is Ingle really free?

Yes. The app is free and Ingle never charges you. The only cost is your group's server, if you rent one: about $4–6 a month from DigitalOcean (or whichever host you pick), billed by them, for the whole group. Hosting it on your own PC is free; the PC just has to stay on.

Does everyone need a server?

No. One person sets up the server, and everyone else installs Ingle and opens the invite link. You can be in as many servers as you like.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is Ingle safe?

Windows shows that for new programs that aren't code-signed yet, and Ingle is new. Click More info, then Run anyway. You don't need to turn anything off.

Want to check first? The code is on GitHub, and the download's SHA-256 is shown after you click Download. After that, updates are checked against Firelit Games' signature.

Who can see our messages?

Calls (voice, cameras, screens and stream sound) are end-to-end encrypted: not even the server can listen. Text messages and pictures are stored on your group's server, like Discord's are on theirs, so whoever runs the server could read them. End-to-end encrypted messages are coming in Ingle 0.8.

Can I use Ingle on a Mac or my phone?

Not yet. Ingle runs on Windows 10 and 11, and on Linux (Ubuntu 22.04 or newer, Linux Mint 21+, Pop!_OS, Debian 12+).

Can we move our server later?

Yes. The owner's Ingle backs the server up to their PC every hour, and a backup sets up the same server on any other host, with all its history. You're never tied to one provider.

How do updates work?

Ingle checks for a new version every few hours (or when you click its version number at the top of the window). When there is one, a green Update button shows up there: click it, and Ingle downloads it, checks Firelit Games' signature, installs it and puts you back in your voice channel.

How do I uninstall it?

On Windows, use Settings → Apps → Ingle → Uninstall. Your identity and settings stay in %APPDATA%\Ingle, in case you come back. On Linux, run sudo apt remove ingle.
